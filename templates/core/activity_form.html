Before you upload

  • Pick the Module this activity belongs to — it's filed under that module automatically.
  • PDF activities open in a built-in viewer, with a download button.
  • HTML activities are only ever offered as a download, never rendered live from this site.
  • Publishing makes an activity visible to every student. A draft is visible only to you and administrators.
