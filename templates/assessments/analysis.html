How to read this page
Marks earned
The share of available marks candidates earned on a question. Under 30% is very hard, 85% or more is easy.
Fully correct
The share of candidates who got every mark on the question.
Discrimination
The average score of the top 27% of candidates minus that of the bottom 27%, on this question. Above 0.3 is good. Near 0 means strong and weak candidates did equally well; below 0 means weak candidates did better, which usually points to a wrong key or a confusing question. Needs at least 10 candidates.
Small groups
With fewer than 5 submitted attempts nothing is flagged, because one or two candidates can swing every figure.
