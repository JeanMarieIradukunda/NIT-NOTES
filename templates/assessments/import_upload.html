2. Switch
A. Connects hosts

Answer: 1-B, 2-A

Section D: Open questions (10 marks)
4. Explain DHCP. (5 marks)
Marking guide: Leases and automatic addressing.
  • Typed numbers, Word or HTML automatic numbering and bullets all work. Matching can also be a two-column table.
  • Answers can sit under each question, in a bold or underlined correct option, or in an “Answer Key” list or table at the end (1. B, 1-B 2-C).
  • Marks written as “(2 marks)” set each question’s weight and can fill in the section marks.
  • Pictures are not imported. Old .doc files must be saved as .docx first.
  • Anything the reader is unsure about is flagged on the next screen, where you can fix missing answers before importing.
